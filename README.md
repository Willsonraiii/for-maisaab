# For My Maisaab (सुशीला) 💕

A special, heartfelt page made with love for **Sushila (Maisaab)**.

> "मृत्यु पर्यन्त, अन्तिम सास सम्म तपाईंसँगै अझ धेरै सुन्दर यादहरू र सम्झनाहरू बनाउन चाहन्छु।" 🌹

## ✨ Features
- 🌹 **Interactive Entrance**: *"Come here Mama (भित्र आउनु मामा)"* portal.
- 🎶 **Soothing Web Audio Synthesizer**: Gentle romantic music box melody playing in the background.
- 📸 **13 Real Memories Gallery**: Polaroid-style photo timeline with touching Nepali captions & zoom preview.
- 🥤 **Cute Water Straw Bottle Widget**: Hydration reminder with interactive sips and heart sparkles.
- 🎀 **Hello Kitty's Sweet Whispers**: Cute encouragement quotes to bring a warm smile.
- 💌 **3 Secret Love Letters**: Clickable envelope surprises.
- 🥺 **The Playful Apology / Forgiveness Challenge**: Playful elusive "नाइँ गर्दिनँ" button that dodges and turns into "हजुर, माफ गरें!" with heart explosions.
